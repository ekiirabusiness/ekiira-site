A different kind of software studio

Your work belongs with you.

Creative work is personal, valuable and often confidential. Your tools should help you do the work—not make you dependent on a rented bundle or ask you to hand over your files.

EKIIRA builds professional software with a local-first mindset, clear product boundaries and practical pricing choices. Use the apps that make sense for you, and keep control of the work you make with them.
