Acceptable Use & Copyright Policy

Effective 2026-09-26 · Rajarshi Mukherjee · Questions: ekiira.business@gmail.com

In short. Pinner is a tool for collecting references, planning shoots and running casting. Using it well means using only what you have the right to use, treating the people in your library with care, obeying the rules of the sites you collect from, and never using Pinner to hurt anyone. Because your library lives on your devices, this responsibility is yours — and it is the reason this policy exists.

1. What this policy is for

1.1 This policy applies to everyone who uses the Software (Pinner Studio, the Android app, the browser extension and the licence service) and forms part of the Terms of Service.

1.2 We do not host or see Your Content. We cannot check what is in your library, and we do not monitor it. That does not make anything permitted: you are responsible for what you save, keep and share with the Software, wherever it is stored.

2. Copyright and images

2.1 Saving is not licensing. Pinner lets you save images from the web as references — for inspiration, mood boards and briefs. Saving an image, or placing it on a board, gives you no right to publish, sell, deliver, print or otherwise use it. If you want to use someone else's image, get a licence or permission from the rights holder first.

2.2 Your own work and client work. Only put into client-facing deliverables (boards, comp cards, casting packages, exports, PDFs, share links) images that you own or are licensed to use for that purpose. If a mood board includes reference images you do not have rights to, mark it as internal and do not publish or send it beyond the people it is meant for.

2.3 Credit and licences. Keep the source, credit and licence terms of images you save — Pinner stores the page address and title for that reason — and respect any attribution or usage limits.

2.4 Watermarks and metadata. Do not remove watermarks, copyright notices or rights-management information from someone else's images, or use Pinner to help others do so.

2.5 Repeat infringement. We may suspend or end the licence of a user who repeatedly infringes copyright (see the Copyright Complaints (DMCA) Procedure).

3. Personal data of other people

3.1 Casting and client management involve other people's personal data: names, photos, contact details, measurements, agency, availability, notes, releases. You must:

  • have a lawful basis for holding each person's data (for example their consent, a contract, or a legitimate interest that is balanced against their rights) and tell them about it as the law requires;
  • collect only what you need, keep it only as long as you need it, and delete it when it is no longer needed or when they lawfully ask you to;
  • keep it secure — use a screen lock and disk encryption, protect exports and share links, and think before you sync to a cloud;
  • not share it beyond what the person expects and has agreed to (for example a client receiving a comp card);
  • honour their rights — access, correction, deletion, objection — within the time your law allows.

3.2 Model releases and consents. Pinner can track releases and their expiry, but the Software is not a legal instrument. Make sure that every person who appears in a shoot has signed a release that covers the way you will use the images (media, territory, term, exclusivity, and any nudity or sensitive use), and that you keep the signed originals.

3.3 Minors. Photograph and store data about children only with the informed consent of a parent or legal guardian, and follow the extra rules that apply where you work (working permits, chaperones, image-use restrictions). Never save or share sexualised images of anyone under 18. This is absolutely prohibited (see §5.1).

3.4 Mature content. Pinner lets you mark images, projects, shoots and talent profiles as nudity / mature content. Such work must be lawful where you are, consensual, and involve only adults who have given explicit written consent. Do not store, exchange or publish it in breach of the law or of any platform's rules, and keep it away from anyone who should not see it (use the marker to search and filter, and never send it to a client or cloud without the person's agreement).

3.5 Adult-content tags and the mature-content detector. You may create your own tags about nudity, underwear or other adult content, and let the auto-tagging add-on learn and suggest them, after you confirm once in the app that: you are an adult; everyone shown is an adult who consented to being photographed and to the use you make of the images; and you will use these tags only to organise, filter or keep such work away from people who should not see it. The optional mature-content add-on flags images that may be adult content and can blur their thumbnails; it runs on your computer and looks at the image, not at who is in it. Neither ever infers a person's racial or ethnic origin, religion, political opinions, sexual orientation, health, age, gender, emotions or looks: those stay blocked whatever you confirm. Nothing here changes §3.3 or §5.1.

4. The sites and services you use with Pinner

4.1 You must follow the terms of every website, platform and API you use with Pinner, including Instagram and Facebook (Meta), Google Maps and Google Drive, Dropbox and Microsoft.

4.2 Scouting and content extraction. The "Scout talent" and content-downloading features operate entirely on your local device. Pinner provides the mechanical utility to read a page you chose to open or share, but you are responsible for your use of it. Use it for individual, human-directed scouting only. Do not use Pinner to collect people's data at scale, to build lists or databases of people who have not agreed to it, to get around a private account or a login wall, to bypass the rate limits, terms of service or copyright protections of platforms such as Instagram or Facebook, or to contact people in ways they have not invited or the platform forbids. Automated, bulk or background collection of personal data is prohibited. If a third-party platform or rights holder pursues a claim based on your extraction activities, §12 of the Terms of Service (indemnity) applies.

4.3 Maps. Pinner reads coordinates from a Google Maps link or menu you choose and stores only the latitude, longitude and place name you attach to a shoot. Do not use Pinner to copy, scrape or cache Google Maps content, or to build a map service from it.

4.4 Cloud services. Use your own accounts, and only within the provider's rules.

5. Things you must never do with Pinner

5.1 Never use the Software to create, store, share or facilitate: child sexual abuse material or any sexualised depiction of a minor (we will report it to the authorities and cooperate fully with investigations); non-consensual intimate imagery ("revenge porn"); content depicting or promoting violence against people or animals; or anything else that is unlawful where you or the people involved are.

5.2 Never use it to harass, stalk, threaten, intimidate, defame, impersonate, discriminate against or track any person; to locate someone who does not want to be found; to invade anyone's privacy; or to expose people's personal data without their agreement.

5.3 Never use it to infringe another person's copyright, trademark, publicity, personality, privacy or other rights.

5.4 Never use it to deceive — for example to pass off references as your own work, or to fabricate releases, credits or licences.

5.5 Never attack, overload, probe or abuse the licence service or any other service; try to defeat licence limits or signatures; share or sell licence keys; or use automated tools to guess keys or activate devices.

5.6 Never use it to distribute malware or to break the law in any way.

6. Reporting a problem

6.1 Copyright. Follow the Copyright Complaints (DMCA) Procedure.

6.2 Anything else — misuse of the licence service, abuse, safety concerns — write to ekiira.business@gmail.com. Because we hold no user content we cannot remove images or data from a user's library, but we can revoke licence keys and we will cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.

6.3 If you find illegal content involving a child, report it directly to your national hotline or police (for example the NCMEC CyberTipline in the United States or the Internet Watch Foundation in the United Kingdom).

7. Consequences

We may warn you, suspend or end your licence and revoke your keys if you breach this policy (see the Terms of Service, §13), and we may report unlawful conduct to the authorities.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the Terms of Service explain how changes are notified.
