Projects, clients and invoices

Run the business side of photography

Every job has a client, a fee, a schedule, a set of pictures and a bill. Pinner Studio keeps them together, so the mood board, the shoot, the payments and the delivery all sit under one project.

Download PinnerAbout Pinner Studio

Projects

A pipeline

A Kanban board from lead to delivered, so you can see what is coming in and what is waiting on you.

Tasks

A task board with due dates and priorities, and a list of what is due soon on the dashboard.

Everything linked

Shoots, mood boards and albums attach to the project, so nothing is hunted for later.

The money

Fee, budget, expenses and payments, with tax, balance and profit worked out for you.

Invoices

Make an invoice as a PDF, or export the figures as CSV. Your currency and tax settings are used.

Pinner works out totals from the figures you enter. It is not a substitute for accounting or tax advice.

Clients and gear

Clients are a lightweight CRM with lifetime value, so you can see who your best clients really are.

Gear is an inventory with insured values and kits, and a CSV export. On a shoot, the gear checklist warns you when the same item is booked twice.

Deliver the work

Exports

To a folder or a ZIP, with resizing, format conversion, watermarks and file-name templates.

Contact sheets

A contact sheet PDF of a selection, ready to send.

Client web gallery

One self-contained web page where the client hearts favourites and copies their selection back to you.

Share links

With a cloud account connected, any export can become a share link, copied to your clipboard.

See the whole business at a glance

The dashboard shows upcoming shoots with golden-hour times, a calendar of shoots, casting sessions, deadlines and tasks, tasks that are due, revenue by month and your recent pins.

The free plan includes 3 active projects. Pinner Pro removes that limit. Items you already have are never locked.
