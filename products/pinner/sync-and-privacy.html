Sync and privacy

Private by design: your library stays on your devices

Photographers handle other people's pictures and personal details. Pinner is built so that we never hold them: your pins, projects and talent records live on your own computer, phone and browser, and they stay in step without a Pinner account and without a server of ours.

What stays on your devices

Pinner Studio

Settings, backups and logs, and a library folder with the database, your images and thumbnails. You choose where the library lives.

The extension

Pins are kept in the browser's own storage. Backups are ZIP files that Pinner Studio can import.

The Android app

Secrets are kept in the Android Keystore, and automatic backup carries only ordinary settings. A backup ZIP never contains tokens or keys.

Three ways the apps stay in step

Same computer

The Chrome extension talks to Pinner Studio over the loopback address, which never leaves your machine. Pairing happens by itself.

Your Wi-Fi

The phone finds your computer on the local network and talks to it over TLS, pinned to your computer's own certificate. You confirm four matching digits with one tap on Allow.

Your own cloud Pro

Every device writes its own change files into one folder in storage you own, so a device can catch up on its own, wherever it is.

Cloud sync through your storage

With Pinner Pro, choose where the folder lives: Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, ownCloud or Synology, or any folder that a sync app already keeps in step, such as iCloud Drive, Syncthing or a NAS share. The last option needs no sign-in at all.

Pinner asks only for its own folder: Google's drive.file access, or the app folders of Dropbox and OneDrive. Every device writes only its own files, so two devices can never overwrite each other. Signing in is done once, and your other devices pick it up.

End-to-end encryption Pro

Turn on encryption and everything Pinner writes to the folder, change files and images alike, is AES-256-GCM ciphertext. The key is derived from a passphrase with scrypt, and the passphrase never leaves your devices.

A lost passphrase cannot be recovered. Nobody, including us, can decrypt your folder without it.

The browser extension's own uploads stay unencrypted so that the extension can read them. Use the local link to Pinner Studio for those if that matters to you.

A password for a library

In Pinner Studio you can lock a library with a password. It asks before the library opens, can lock itself after a while, and a recovery key sets a new password if you forget it. Phones give it once. This is not encryption: it keeps the library closed in Pinner, but anyone who can open the library's folder can still read its files. For files in your cloud, turn on end-to-end encryption.

What is on our side, and what is not

  • Pins, images, projects, talent and clients: never. They are not sent to us.
  • Licence checks (Pinner Pro): a small service keeps a hashed licence key with its plan and the names of your devices, and signs a token that your apps check offline. It expires by itself after 24 months without use.
  • Cloud sign-in: a small relay keeps the app's secret key so that no secret ships inside the apps. It keeps nothing and never sees your files.
  • Payments: handled by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record. We never see your card details.
  • Tracking: no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, on this site or in the apps.

The full details are in the privacy policy. To remove your data, see delete your data.

The connection is protected, too

Pinner Studio's local link accepts only programs on your computer and paired devices. It refuses web pages, checks the address it was called by and hands the shared token only to programs on that very computer. A browser on another computer connects through a Tailscale address or an https tunnel that you choose.

Everything you need to start is free, with no time limit and no account. Add Pinner Pro when you want cloud sync and no limits.
