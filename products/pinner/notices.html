5. Data sources and services

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — place search is provided by the OpenStreetMap Foundation's Nominatim service; results are © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Pinner sends a search only when you look up a place, and identifies itself as required by the Nominatim usage policy.
  • Sun and moon positions are calculated on your device from published astronomical formulae. Weather: when the automatic place lookup is on, the shoot's coordinates and date are sent to a forecast service to fetch a probable-weather note; no account or identifier goes with it. The service and its licence are named in the Privacy Policy.
