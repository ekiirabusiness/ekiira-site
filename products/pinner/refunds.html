Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 2026-09-26 · Rajarshi Mukherjee · Questions: ekiira.business@gmail.com

In short. Pinner is free to try, so you can see whether it suits you before you pay. If you buy Pinner Pro and it isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days (30 days for the lifetime licence) and you will get one. You can cancel a subscription at any time; you keep Pro until the end of what you paid for.

1. Who sells Pinner Pro

Purchases are made through Dodo Payments ("Dodo"), the merchant of record. Dodo takes the payment, charges and pays any sales tax or GST, and issues your receipt and licence key. Refunds are paid by Dodo to the payment method you used. Dodo's own terms apply to the sale; this policy tells you what we will do.

2. Money-back guarantee

2.1 Subscriptions (monthly and yearly). If you are not satisfied with your first purchase, request a refund within fourteen (14) days of the payment and we will refund it in full.

2.2 Lifetime licence. Request a refund within thirty (30) days of the payment and we will refund it in full. As set out in the Terms of Service (§4.6), no refund is given after that window if Pinner Pro is later discontinued.

2.3 Renewals. A renewal payment is refunded in full if you ask within seven (7) days of it being charged and you have not used Pro since the renewal on a new device or for new work — please cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged. (The reminder email from Dodo tells you the date.)

2.4 We may refuse a refund where there are signs of abuse — for example repeated purchase-and-refund cycles, a key used on more devices than allowed, or a chargeback filed for the same payment.

3. Your statutory rights

3.1 If you are a consumer in the EU, the UK or another place with a statutory right to cancel a distance contract within 14 days, you have that right; you can use it by telling us or Dodo within 14 days of the purchase. By activating a licence key you ask us to start providing the digital content immediately; where the law allows, you then give up the statutory right to withdraw once the content has been supplied — but our money-back guarantee in §2 still applies to you.

3.2 Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under consumer law, including to a remedy if the Software is faulty or is not as described.

4. How to cancel a subscription

4.1 Open Settings → Licence → Manage subscription in Pinner Studio, the extension or the Android app (it opens your billing page at Dodo), or use the link in any receipt email from Dodo.

4.2 Cancellation stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for; nothing is refunded for the unused part of a period unless §2 or the law says otherwise, or the Terms of Service (§9.2, changes that reduce a plan) give you a pro-rata refund.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email ekiira.business@gmail.com from the address you bought with (or give your licence key or the receipt number), or ask Dodo through the billing page. We will confirm, and Dodo will return the money to your original payment method, usually within five to ten (5–10) business days depending on your bank.

6. What happens after a refund or cancellation

6.1 The licence key is deactivated when a payment is refunded, reversed or a subscription ends. Pro features pause on your devices at their next licence check (or after the offline grace period described in the Terms of Service, §4.8).

6.2 Your data is not affected. Everything you created stays on your devices and in your own cloud account. Cloud sync stops when Pro ends; the free plan's limits then apply to creating new projects, shoots, boards and talent — existing items stay available to view, edit and export.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank — we would rather fix it. A chargeback may cause the licence to be suspended while it is resolved.

8. Prices and taxes

Prices are shown at checkout in your local currency where Dodo Payments offers it, and include or exclude tax as the checkout shows. We tell you before a subscription price changes, and the new price applies only from the following renewal.
