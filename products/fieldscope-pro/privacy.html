PRIVACY / ON SET

The footage stays with you.

FieldScope Pro is designed to process monitoring, camera, audio and recording data on your Android device. It does not require a user account and does not include ads or analytics SDKs.

Read the overview ↓Privacy questions

What stays local

Your media is not a telemetry stream.

These are the product's stated data-handling principles. They do not replace the final legal privacy notice shown with a release.

NO ACCOUNT

No sign-up to monitor

FieldScope Pro does not require an EKIIRA user account to use the app.

NO TRACKING SDK

No ads or analytics

The app brief states that there are no ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or automatic crash uploads.

ON DEVICE

Media stays on your device

Captured video, audio and files are stored locally under your device and storage choices.

LOCAL REPORTS

Crash detail stays local

A last-crash report is kept locally in the app; sharing or clearing it is user initiated.

NO LOCATION

Photos exclude location data

Location is not written into the app's camera photos.

BILLING

Google Play purchase checks

Google Play Billing communicates with Google to process and restore a Pro purchase.

Network features are deliberate

You choose where the feed goes.

  • Streaming is off by defaultIt does not begin unless the user starts it.
  • LAN viewing uses your networkBrowser viewers and monitor receivers connect across a network you control; FieldScope Pro does not relay those feeds through an EKIIRA cloud server.
  • External destinations are yours to selectIf you configure an RTMP/RTMPS destination, the stream is sent to that destination.
  • Permissions are tied to useUSB, camera, microphone, storage picker, network and foreground-service permissions are requested for related app features.
