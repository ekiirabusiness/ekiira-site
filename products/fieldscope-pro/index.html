From camera to monitor

Connect. Check. Capture or share.

Use a compatible USB HDMI capture device for external cameras, or open the built-in camera and bring monitoring tools along.

  1. ConnectPlug in a supported USB UVC HDMI capture device, or choose a phone camera, clip or test pattern.
  2. MonitorSet exposure, focus, framing, LUTs and scopes for the image in front of you.
  3. RecordCapture clips and stills locally. Recording options depend on device support and license.
  4. Share locallyIn Pro, stream to a viewer on your network or an endpoint you select.

An honest tool for real devices

Work with the hardware you have.

  • No proprietary capture boxUse a supported UVC USB HDMI capture card and an Android device with USB host/OTG.
  • No account, ads or analytics SDKsYour camera feeds and working files stay on-device unless you choose a stream destination.
  • Capabilities vary by phoneCamera controls, RAW, 10-bit formats and capture-card modes depend on the device, lens, Android version and accessory.
