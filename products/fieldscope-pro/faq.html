Setup and compatibility

Does FieldScope Pro need an HDMI capture card?

No. The phone's own cameras, test patterns, local clips and supported network streams are also inputs. Use a compatible USB UVC HDMI capture card to monitor an external camera.

Will every USB capture card work?

No. It needs to expose a supported UVC video stream. Capture devices that require a vendor-specific Android driver are not supported. USB link speed and the card's output mode affect practical resolution and frame rate.

Does my phone need OTG?

An Android device with USB host/OTG support is needed for USB HDMI capture. Camera-only mode does not require a capture card.

Does it support HDMI input built into a TV box?

Android's native TV Input Framework HDMI-in is not generally accessible to third-party apps. A box that exposes its input to Android as a supported external camera may be usable.

Will every phone have manual camera controls and RAW?

No. The app uses Android Camera2 capability information, which varies by device and lens. Some devices limit manual controls or RAW output, especially on secondary cameras.

Can every phone record real 10-bit or 4:2:2 video?

No. Format availability depends on the camera path, Android version and hardware encoder. Phone camera feeds are 8-bit today except for supported HLG10 camera paths; phone-camera 4:2:2 is interpolated chroma. Input cards can provide 10-bit P010 where supported.

Is RAW video available?

No. RAW video is on the roadmap. RAW still photos in DNG format are a separate camera feature for compatible lenses.

What is the streaming latency?

HLS is approximately three seconds. WebRTC and other paths depend on the device, network and selected settings. The streaming feature is intended to use your network or an endpoint you provide, not EKIIRA's cloud.

Can I use the app offline?

The app does not require an account. After a Pro purchase has been validated, Pro features are designed to work offline.

Is Pro a subscription?

The Pro upgrade is described as a one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase, restorable across devices and usable offline after purchase. Current price and availability come from the store listing.

Does FieldScope Pro collect usage or crash data?

The product brief states that there are no analytics, ads, tracking SDKs or automatic crash uploads. A local crash report can be shared by the user.

What about battery and heat?

Thermal forecasting and battery status can trigger automatic power saving. Redraw and standby behavior are designed to reduce load when possible, but long camera, monitor and recording sessions still depend on the device.
