SETUP / COMPATIBILITY

Bring HDMI in. Put the phone to work.

FieldScope Pro uses compatible UVC USB capture hardware as the input path for an external camera. No capture card? The built-in phone camera, test patterns, local clips and supported network inputs still work.

Connect a camera ↓Common questions

Three steps

Connect the camera feed.

Start with an Android device with USB host support and a UVC-compatible capture card. Capture cards and cables are third-party hardware.

  1. Connect HDMIRoute the camera's HDMI output to a compatible UVC USB capture device.
  2. Connect USBAttach the capture device to the Android phone or tablet using USB host/OTG as needed.
  3. Grant accessApprove the USB-device prompt and select the input when the app opens.
  4. Check the signalReview resolution, frame rate, pixel format and bandwidth in the signal information.
Capture hardware is not included. USB HDMI capture cards differ in formats, bandwidth and driver support. Confirm the card works as UVC hardware with your device.

UVC capture compatibility

Match the card to the USB link.

Transfer mode and input format affect the resolution and frame rate the phone can sustain. The app reports signal details and can explain unsupported hardware.

Connection typeBriefed exampleCompatibility note
USB 2 captureMJPEGUp to about 1080p30, depending on the capture device and link.
USB 3 captureUncompressed YUY2 or NV121080p60 on compatible hardware and a capable USB 3 link.
USB 3 captureMJPEG4K30 on compatible hardware; decoding load and device performance vary.
10-bit captureP010Depends on capture device, USB bandwidth, Android device and end-to-end format support.
Unsupported hardware: cards that depend on a vendor-specific driver are not supported. Native Android HDMI-in ports are not directly accessible to third-party apps; a device exposing HDMI through a supported external-camera API may differ.

Feature & device support

The app should tell you what the phone can do.

  • Camera controls vary by lensAvailable sensor, focus, RAW and encoder capabilities differ across phone models and even between lenses on one phone.
  • Controls are capability-awareUnsupported options are hidden or explained rather than silently shown as if every camera can use them.
  • OpenGL ES 3.1Required for the FieldScope Pro rendering and scope pipeline.
  • Power and thermals matterThermal and battery conditions can trigger automatic power-saving behavior. Recording needs the app in the foreground.
