PRIVACY / UNRELEASED WORK

Your script is your intellectual property.

ScreenplayX is built as a local-first desktop suite. Keep unproduced scripts and production databases on your own machine, without requiring a cloud workspace to write.

Read the principles ↓Privacy FAQ

Private by default

The project lives on your computer.

Story development can carry confidential ideas, client information and unannounced production details. The product's architecture is designed around local storage and local work.

LOCAL DATABASE

Project data stays on-device

Screenplays and connected project databases are stored locally on your desktop hardware.

NO CLOUD SCRAPING

Your script is not training material

ScreenplayX does not use your creative intellectual property to train AI models.

WORK OFFLINE

Writing is not tied to sign-in

Activation can use online services; you do not need an always-on connection just to write a script.

License activation

License checks are separate from your project.

  • Signed license tokensLicense activation is described as using RSA-signed tokens and hardware binding.
  • Activation endpointsLicense activation can contact services used for validation. That is distinct from saving and editing a local screenplay.
  • Secure checkoutDodo Payments is the Merchant of Record for secure checkout and global licensing.
