01 / The writer's room

Build the script without breaking the flow.

Write in a familiar screenplay structure while keeping an eye on the narrative choices that shape the draft.

SCRIPT EDITOR

Industry-standard formatting

Write scenes, action, dialogue and transitions in a screenplay editor designed for focused drafting.

STRUCTURE

Use story frameworks as guides

Overlay Three-Act Structure, The Hero's Journey or Save the Cat story beats to help inspect pacing.

UNDO

Recover more than text edits

The undo system is designed to track granular changes as writing and project structure evolve.

02 / The director's vision

Visualize moments before the camera rolls.

Keep beats, references and framing ideas beside the scenes they describe. Reorganize a story visually without losing its connection to the script.

  • Beat boardsArrange story beats on a digital board and link them to screenplay scenes.
  • Visual storyboardsAttach reference images, sketches and lighting notes to scene headers.
  • Flexible layoutsArrange the editor, beat board and storyboard beside one another when planning.

03 / The producer's desk

Connect creative choices to practical prep.

Keep a production database alongside the script, then organize how the story can be made.

BREAKDOWN

Keep track of production elements

Manage characters, locations, props, talent and wardrobe in relation to scenes.

SHOT PLANNING

Detail the visual coverage

Attach camera angles, lens choices and movement notes to scene-specific shot lists.

SCHEDULE

Organize the shooting order

Arrange scene strips into a schedule and surface logic warnings, including missing locations or double-booked talent.
