About ScreenplayX

What is ScreenplayX?

A native desktop pre-production suite that connects screenplay editing with beat boards, storyboarding, shot planning, shoot scheduling and a production database.

What platforms does it support?

Windows is the current desktop target. A macOS build is planned. This is not a browser-based web app, and no iOS version is claimed.

Where are my scripts stored?

Project files and the SQLite project database are stored locally on the desktop machine.

Do I need to be online to write?

No. License activation can use remote endpoints, but an always-on connection is not required for writing.

Are screenplay scenes linked to the boards and plan?

Yes. The product is designed to connect scenes with beats, visual storyboards, shot lists, production data and the schedule through one project database.

Which story structures are included?

The editor brief describes visual guides based on the Three-Act Structure, The Hero's Journey and Save the Cat.

Is there an EKIIRA bundle?

No. EKIIRA products are offered individually. ScreenplayX has annual and lifetime options described in the product brief.

What does the lifetime license include?

The described lifetime license covers the current major version. Confirm any major-version upgrade terms at purchase.

How does regional pricing work?

Purchasing Power Parity pricing is described for supported regions, including India, Brazil and Indonesia. Final amounts depend on the purchase location.

Who processes the purchase?

Dodo Payments is the Merchant of Record for secure checkout and global licensing.

What are the minimum system requirements?

Detailed minimum hardware requirements were not supplied for this product website. Windows is the current platform target; exact requirements should be confirmed with the release information.

Where can I contact support?

A support email has not been supplied for publication yet. The contact information should be added here when EKIIRA finalizes it.
